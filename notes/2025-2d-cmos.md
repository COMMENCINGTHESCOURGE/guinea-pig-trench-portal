# Technical Note: Substrate-Level Boundaries & Minimal Primitives in 2D CMOS

**Published / Tracked by:** GUINEA-PIG-TRENCH
**Primary Reference:** Ghosh, S. et al. *"A complementary two-dimensional material-based one instruction set computer."* *Nature* (Published online 11 June 2025). DOI: [10.1038/s41586-025-08963-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08963-7)
**Synthesis Facility:** 2D Crystal Consortium – Materials Innovation Platform (2DCC-MIP, NSF-funded), Pennsylvania State University. Growth recipes and characterization data: [doi.org/10.26207/trxj-sj92](https://doi.org/10.26207/trxj-sj92)

---

### 1. The Verified Milestone
Researchers at Pennsylvania State University (Das Laboratory) demonstrated a complete complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor (CMOS) computing architecture fabricated entirely from 2D transition metal dichalcogenides:

* **Channel Composition:** Monolayer Molybdenum Disulfide ($\text{MoS}_2$) for n-type FETs and bilayer Tungsten Diselenide ($\text{WSe}_2$) for p-type FETs, synthesized via wafer-scale Metal-Organic Chemical Vapor Deposition (MOCVD) at the 2DCC-MIP facility.
* **Scale & Integration:** Integrated $>1{,}000$ transistors of each polarity on-chip.
* **Execution & Profile:** Configured as a One-Instruction-Set Computer (OISC) executing deterministic baseline logic operations at $\sim 25\text{ kHz}$ with low supply voltage, enabled by precise threshold-voltage ($V_{\text{th}}$) matching via fabrication and post-processing.

While clock frequencies remain deliberately modest compared to mature bulk silicon, the device validates functional, integrated CMOS logic on atomically thin channels without bulk electrostatic breakdown.

---

### 2. Engineering Parallels & Relevance

#### A. Correctness at the Physical Boundary vs. Post-Hoc Mitigation
Silicon scaling at sub-2 nm regimes encounters severe electrostatic breakdown and quantum leakage—problems historically addressed through geometric workarounds (FinFETs, Gate-All-Around nanosheets) and thermal compensations.

The 2D CMOS approach solves boundary degradation at the **substrate definition layer**: atomically uniform 2D crystal lattices maintain intrinsic gate electrostatics down to monolayer/bilayer thicknesses.

* **The Parallel:** This directly reflects our design stance across the `MANIFOLD` and `Aetherion` projects. Numerical stability and physical invariants are enforced directly within the underlying field definitions and geometric boundary conditions, rather than relying on dampeners, clamping, or post-hoc error correction.

#### B. Minimal-Primitive Determinism (OISC & Discrete Solvers)
An OISC demonstrates computational completeness using a single primitive instruction executed deterministically across hardware.

* **The Parallel:** This cleanly parallels our discrete solver architectures (e.g., integer sieves and primitive rule-space exploration in ARC-AGI). Rather than relying on sprawling instruction sets or probabilistic heuristics, complete emergent behavior is derived and exhaustively verified from minimal, exact primitives.

#### C. Integer-Exact Evaluation on Constrained Fabrics
A $25\text{ kHz}$ clock frequency is impractical for high-throughput rasterization or general-purpose operating systems, but it is fully sufficient for discrete workloads where **state certainty and reproducibility outweigh clock frequency**.

For formal verification pipelines, exact integer sieves, and invariant-checking engines, a deterministic low-power substrate that eliminates leakage-induced state corruption provides a coherent physical model for exact computation.
