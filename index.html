THE GATE

The # is a sluice gate. Tilt it and it becomes ♯ — the sharp sign. Same four bars, different meaning depending on where you stand. One structure, multiple readings, position determines signal. That's the whole architecture.

Everything here runs one algorithm: tip → steer → deposit. A river starts at a peak, follows the gradient, deposits sediment at sea level. A hypha spawns at the surface, steers toward signal, contracts at the core. A number enters the sieve, gets filtered by residues, survives or doesn't. A chord voice-leads through Z₁₂, minimizing displacement, landing on resolution. A beat hits -14 LUFS — the Egyptian zero of streaming — and passes through the gate.

Same operation. Different substrates. The code written for one domain transfers to all others. The fungi propagation engine IS the chord interpolator IS the sieve filter IS the lava flow. Not a metaphor. The Erdős-Straus sieve and the Thomas attractor maze share a measured spectral correlation of r = 0.444 at p < 10⁻¹¹. The math connects them. We just listened.
